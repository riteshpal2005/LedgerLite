# v0.0.1

Welcome to LedgerLite! This is the first official release of your new favorite personal finance and expense tracking companion. LedgerLite is designed to be lightning-fast, privacy-focused, and completely functional even when you don't have an internet connection.

### ✨ What's Inside

- **Instant "Quick Add" Shortcut:** Add a transaction in less than a second right from your phone's home screen without even opening the full app.
- **Always Available (Offline-First):** Your data lives on your device. You don't need an internet connection to log expenses, view charts, or check your balances.
- **Beautiful Visuals & Themes:** Enjoy a stunning, modern interface with support for Light Mode, Dark Mode, and a battery-saving Pitch-Black mode.
- **Smart Categories:** Organize your spending effortlessly. Includes special categories like "Self Transfers" (moving money between your own accounts) and flags for "Suspicious UPI Deductions". Uncategorized entries are safely excluded from your total calculations.
- **Interactive Analytics:** Understand your spending habits at a glance with beautiful, interactive charts and a powerful filtering menu.
- **Running Balances:** Instantly see your account balance after every single transaction, perfectly calculated even if you add backdated expenses.
- **Data Freedom (Import & Export):** Easily import your old transaction history from Excel or CSV files in bulk. Export your current data to Excel or generate beautiful PDF reports to share.
- **Secure Cloud Backup:** Enjoy peace of mind with secure, automatic background backups. If you get a new phone, your data is safe and ready to sync.
- **Built-in App Updates:** Seamlessly download and install new app updates directly from within LedgerLite, without ever having to manage messy download folders.
